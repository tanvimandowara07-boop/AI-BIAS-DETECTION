First row must be column headers. Each row is one decision about one person.

Method: for each attribute, groups are compared on the rate of the favourable outcome. The disparity ratio is the lowest group rate divided by the highest, and the bias score is (1 − ratio) × 100. A ratio under 0.80 fails the four-fifths rule. Groups under 5 rows are ignored. This shows statistical disparity, not its cause, and is not legal advice.
